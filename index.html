WHO THE FUCK?

"Artist from OKC just clowning around ~ making art & eating the leftovers outta ur fridge."

DumpsterFacial is an Oklahoma City-based multidisciplinary artist and DJ whose work blurs the line between music, performance, drag, fashion, visual art and absolute fucking nonsense.

Her visual world pulls from trailer-park culture, urban street life, rave aesthetics, punk, glitch art, clown imagery and DIY culture.

Under the broader TRA$H CULT / RED40CORE universe, music and visual identity become one giant, deliberately dysfunctional collage.

DumpsterFacial's work has historically centered around experimental electronic music and performance, including her 2022 debut EP Bimbo Affirmations.

Her current DJ identity leans heavily into multigenre electronic music, EDM, club sounds, industrial textures, punk energy and whatever the fuck sounds good at maximum volume.

The goal isn't to fit neatly into a genre.

THE GOAL IS TO MAKE THE ROOM WEIRDER.
